I'm a senior engineer who's spent the last decade helping teams build maintainable products — backends for student information systems, neobanks, and most recently a regulatory-intelligence platform for the US energy sector.

I run Equinox Solutions, an independent consultancy. Engagements are usually senior-shaped: architecture review, data-platform builds, embedded technical leadership for product teams that want senior judgment without senior overhead.
