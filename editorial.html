§ About

Dal Lake, Srinagar — 2024

I'm Vijith — a senior engineer who's spent the last decade quietly making other people's code easier to work with. I've built backends for student information systems, neobanks, and now regulatory data infrastructure for the US energy sector.

My favourite engagements are the ones where the codebase is almost right. A team that knows what it's trying to build, but the wiring has accumulated a few too many decisions made under pressure. I come in, listen for a few weeks, and then quietly start removing things until the shape of the product is visible again.

I work across Ruby on Rails, Python, Go, and Java — but the language is rarely the interesting part. What matters is observable systems, defensible data models, and teams that trust each other enough to ship every week.
